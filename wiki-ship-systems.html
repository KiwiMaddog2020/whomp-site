Ship systems

There is one socket per core, laid out in the order the campaign fills them, with the memory archive at the centre of the ring instead of out on it. As the cores come back the wreck stops looking like a wreck, in 4 stages. Every count of cores you could be holding from 0 through 10 is written down against the stage it puts you at.

Fragments do not seat in these sockets. A fragment names a system, and a socket names a system, and the game never says the one goes into the other.

How the wreck comes back

The rebuild, stage by stage

Tier 1 at 1 cores

First light

Two repair bays wear fresh alloy against the weathered skin, the doorway gets a threshold plate, and one conduit relights along the flank.

Tier 2 at 3 cores

The spine holds

Five bays panelled, the deck plates re-seated over the two holes inside, a second conduit run lit the length of the hull.

Tier 3 at 6 cores

She stands

Eight bays panelled, the dorsal rent patched over, the snapped strut braced back under her, and the navigation lamp stops guttering and holds.

Tier 4 at 9 cores

Ready to answer

Every bay bright, the fin crack splinted, both nacelle cowls re-hung, the ramp re-treaded, the pad cleats catching light, and the light inside turns warm.
