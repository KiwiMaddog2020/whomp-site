Weapon tiers

33 of 33 weapons are measured across 83 form-and-level rows. Each letter is relative to its artifact-defined cohort, so rows from different cohorts are never ranked against one another.

Every value on this page is read from a verified generated artifact at build time, against game@d2b39395. The generator refuses stale artifacts, missing domains, missing entries and dead relations before it writes a page. Source: data/tier-rankings.json · fingerprint 869e0ddba75a8334eb2a8850c45bece3.

Tomes, relics and characters are named as unmeasured by the artifact. Tomes: A tome has no standalone output; its number is a DELTA against a reference build, and choosing that reference is a design decision this lane was not scoped to make. Named follow-on lane. Relics: Same shape as tomes, plus a chest-economy term. Named follow-on lane. Characters: Confounded by innate + signature + starting weapon at once; not separable on this rig.

Measured, not voted

Artifact-defined jobs and tiers

Collapsing crowd-clear and boss damage into one score needs a weight, and a weight is an opinion. Two letters is the honest publication.

Trash clearkills per minute against 72 respawning 35-HP enemies that pressure an orbiting playerkills/min

Survival is the metric a run cares about and it is NOT reachable per weapon: src/dev/headlessSim.ts has no per-weapon damage attribution (damageByWeapon is keyed by source phase), no starting-weapon override, and its draft puts four weapons in the run within minutes. Locking the others out does not isolate either, because partitionLockedOut falls back to the held ids when a pool empties. Reported as a correct negative rather than faked.
