Automatic weapons, measured

38 of the 45 automatic weapons went through the same two tests, at 102 combinations of form and level. This is a laboratory and not a run: nothing here dodges, nothing here is aimed, and a letter only says where a row landed against the other rows it was compared with. Where a test could not honestly produce a number, the row says so instead of taking a guess.

Core weapons, tomes, relics and characters have no letters here, and the measurement says why. Tomes: A tome has no standalone output; its number is a DELTA against a reference build, and choosing that reference is a design decision this lane was not scoped to make. Named follow-on lane. Relics: Relics span direct stats, economy, survival, movement and event effects. No universal reference build or grade was selected. Characters: Confounded by innate + signature + starting weapon at once; not separable on this rig. Core weapons: Campaign cores are aimed/manual-control weapons with a separate runtime registry. This automatic WeaponSystem orbit cannot measure human aim.

How the measuring was done

The tests, and what they hold still

Collapsing crowd-clear and boss damage into one score needs a weight, and a weight is an opinion. Two letters is the honest publication.

Survival is the metric a run cares about and it is NOT reachable per weapon: src/dev/headlessSim.ts has no per-weapon damage attribution (damageByWeapon is keyed by source phase), no starting-weapon override, and its draft puts four weapons in the run within minutes. Locking the others out does not isolate either, because partitionLockedOut falls back to the held ids when a pool empties. Reported as a correct negative rather than faked.

Worker count changes wall-clock execution only. The CLI disables V8 Maglev before simulation, fixture workers inherit that process policy, and seeded tasks return in canonical order; numeric evidence and fingerprint are worker-count invariant. Workers used: 4 of cap 4; execution-only, not measurement evidence.

  • This ranks bare automatic WeaponSystem weapons, not aimed core weapons and not full-run builds. A weapon that scales with stats is measured without those stats.
  • The rig is WeaponSystem + Projectiles + GroundPatches. No director, elites, boss attacks/phases, player damage, survival, XP or draft economy.
  • The trash fixture respawns on death, so kills/min measures throughput and is not capped by a finite population.
  • The boss-class fixture is stationary and stands 5.1-7.9m from the orbiting player. It does not execute authored boss movement, attacks, or phases; this is target-damage evidence, not boss-fight performance.
  • A weapon whose reach is shorter than the controlled target distance measures ZERO there, and that zero is a fact about this fixture as much as about the weapon.
  • Echo's wake does not intersect the stationary boss-class fixture. Those bossDamage rows are explicitly UNMEASURED and receive no tier or rank.
  • Order statistics are discrete, never interpolated: quantiles use the nearest zero-based sample index round((n - 1) * q), and even-sized medians use the upper middle sample.
  • Measured controlled-sim builds are greedy four-weapon chains, not exhaustive optima or live-play recommendations.
  • Greedy build selection and reported build performance use the same deterministic seed cohort; there is no holdout cohort, so selection bias is possible.
  • Multi-weapon rows use a fixed lexicographic insertion order. Other acquisition orders are not measured and can change cast interleave.
