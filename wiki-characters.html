Wiki/Heroes/Characters

Characters

A character is a starting health number, a speed, a damage multiplier, one passive rule and one signature move. The three numbers are inputs the run multiplies, not the speed you travel at or the damage you land, and the weapon named on a card is a suggestion rather than something you walk in holding.

Runtime interpretation

Loadout suggestion and authored identity inputs

startWeaponId is a character loadout suggestion and Battle/build identity, not an unconditional campaign starting pickup.

The standard solo campaign begins with the aimed core only; its first automatic weapon comes from the live draft.

Other synthetic and multiplayer contexts apply their own explicit loadout or world grant policy.
