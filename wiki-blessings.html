Shrine blessings

A shrine you activate offers three, drawn from these 19. That trio is only part of what a shrine can do, because a legendary replacement can turn up ahead of the blessing menu and a way to move can follow it, so this page is not the complete set of shrine outcomes.

What lighting one up actually does

Blessings first, then a legendary or a way to move

A normal world shrine that lights up has 1 movement slot to fill, and it only fills if no legendary replacement arrived first. Directive and merchant offers do not carry that slot at all.

Every activated world shrine rolls its blessing trio, then fills the eligible movement slot from this complete registry.

Directive and merchant blessing offers do not include the movement slot. Nothing else can pre-empt it: director ruling 26 (2026-08-08) cut the legendary roll that used to replace the whole menu, so a world activation always reaches the movement draw.

Offerings at their maxStacks ceiling leave the eligible pool; Extra Jump is uncapped, so the pool cannot become empty.

JUMP_AUGMENTS is a legacy card/progression alias surface for Spring Step and Extra Jump, not the complete shrine movement pool.
