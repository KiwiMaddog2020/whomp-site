2026-08-05 v0.6.2 written for the log

A week of releases, caught up in one entry

This log went quiet for six days while three releases shipped. That is the gap, and this entry closes it. From today each release writes its own entry here as it is cut, so a week like this one cannot go unrecorded again.

The three are 0.6.0 on July 31, 0.6.1 on August 4, and 0.6.2 today. The two older ones have their own entries below this one. This entry is the week.

New

  • Two snow worlds are reachable. Frostreach Pines and Shiverpeak Summit sit behind the Frost Gate, so a run now goes hearth, desert, frost, void. They borrow desert enemies for this release while their own roster is built.
  • Build tracks. Stable is the recommended weekly build, Preview is the latest fully green one. Each installs as its own app with its own on-device progress, and online rooms now match on both track and exact build, so a Stable player and a Preview player cannot start a divergent match by accident.
  • Your core weapon reads more of your build. The scattergun and the longbow respond to victims, arc and area, so four upgrade paths that previously did nothing for them now change how they fire. Cores also declare an element, and committing a build to one sharpens what that element does rather than handing you a flat bonus.
  • Duels and arena matches end on a scope-out screen. Both sides, side by side, with what each was running and how the fight actually went.

Better

  • Mouse look is the default for mouse and keyboard. The camera turns the way it does in a shooter and the cursor stays parked above your character. There is a Cursor Height slider in Settings, and click to aim is still under Controls.
  • Starfall no longer lands in the opening minutes. The first shard falls after five minutes and the crown after nine, so an early lucky pickup cannot decide the run.

Fixed

  • Duels stop dragging. Lifesteal and health regeneration were both measured against unscaled health while every damage number in a duel was scaled, so the safety cap never bound and every seat healed at the same flat rate whatever it drafted. Both are fixed, and in testing every stalemate resolved.
  • The laser telegraphs its return sweep. The second pass used to arrive with no warning, which made a readable attack read as a random one.
  • Co-op runs have sound. Hits, pickups, level ups and everything else were silent for every player in a co-op game.
  • The challenge bell and its reward drop were silent and now are not.
  • The snow levels no longer grow meadow clover and gold flowers on the ice.
  • The village has cats.

Coming

  • The snow tier gets enemies of its own, so the frost worlds stop borrowing from the desert.
  • A boss to close the snow tier out.
2026-08-04 v0.6.1 from the release notes

The Whomp Lab becomes a private director workspace, and new Stable and Preview tracks make it safer to try the latest green build.

Highlights

  • Choose your build track from the title screen. Stable is the recommended weekly build; Preview is the latest fully green build. Each is its own installable PWA with separate on-device progress, and switching always goes to one fixed, trusted WHOMP address.
  • The Whomp Lab is reorganized into Explore, Practice, Review and QA. Seven taps on THE WHOMP LAB reveal a session-only Director Console with the local Studio Board and a copy-and-paste decision workspace for coordinating development without putting credentials or remote execution inside the game.
  • Online rooms now match both the build track and exact build. A Stable player and a Preview player cannot accidentally start a divergent duel or co-op run, and the lobby explains how to get everyone onto the same track.
  • Preview is intentionally low-risk: it keeps cloud saves and public leaderboard writes on Stable while still sending clearly labelled feedback, crash reports and play-session evidence so the newest build can improve quickly.

Fixed

  • Level-up velocity offers no longer appear when none of your live weapons can use them.
  • Account name dialogs can be escaped cleanly, keep keyboard focus available, and ignore stale results from an earlier modal session.
  • Preview no longer offers account sign-in or cloud-save controls that it cannot safely use.
  • Cross-track online mismatches now fail closed instead of retrying an incompatible room.

2026-07-30 v0.5.0 written for the log

Duels stop dragging, and sign-in shows up

If you have played the arena the last few days, you already know the problem: fights that should end in fifteen or twenty seconds were stretching on and on. Today's build tracks down why and fixes it for good.

Fixed

  • Lifesteal was way too strong in duels. The safety cap that keeps lifesteal from getting silly worked fine in a normal run, but it never kicked in inside the arena, so a player who stacked lifesteal could out-heal almost anything you threw at them. That cap now works the way it always should have.
  • Health regen had the exact same bug. Every fighter was passively healing at the same flat rate no matter what they drafted, and in a fast arena fight that rate covers a huge share of the incoming damage. Regen now scales with the fight too, so building for sustain finally means something, and not building for it finally costs something.
  • Stalemates are gone. With both bugs fixed together, we ran a big batch of test duels and every single stalemate resolved. Fights land where they are supposed to: fast enough to feel sharp, long enough that a good hit still matters.

Better

  • Some of the passive damage sources (auras, ground effects, homing shots) got turned down a notch, so a clean hit you land or take actually reads as a hit instead of getting buried under chip damage.

New

  • Sign-in is live. There is a real Google sign-in now, in Settings, and it is the same account across the game and this site. It is early: right now it remembers who you are and shows your cloud save status. More is coming once there is something worth signing in for.

Coming

  • More PvP tuning is already queued behind this fix. We want a good read and a clean hit to matter without turning every duel into a coinflip.
  • Accounts unlocking cross-device saves and a real leaderboard are next.
2026-07-20 v0.4.0 from the release notes

Heroic Leap joins the kit, multiplayer duels get a real feel pass, and a big craft sweep across movement, depth, and sprites.

Highlights

  • The full standard control kit is ready from the start: Boost, WHOMP, your character signature, and Heroic Leap on F. Seven alternate F utilities remain separate unlocks in the Arsenal Forge: Recall Rune, Second Wind, Bulwark, Boop, Hook, Stasis, and Chasm.
  • Multiplayer duels got a full feel pass: your HUD now carries into the ring, phase changes show loading screens instead of hard freezes, a pre-duel 3-2-1 countdown and a round score card frame each bout, a dropped opponent hands you the win, and the top-left score readout was cleaned up. Hitting Escape mid-duel now opens the menu without freezing your opponent, and online lobbies support up to four players in a free-for-all beta.
  • Movement and traversal polish: the walk gait speeds up toward a jog and scales with your speed, jump pads launch twice as high, pits are deeper with pitch-black bottoms that no longer catch stray shadows from above, and paired in-run portals let you stumble onto a teleport across the map.
  • A craft sweep on how things sit in the world: ground rings (starfield, aura, jump-landing dust) always render above the ground yet under the trees, rings centered on you ride your jump and pass correctly over the trees when you are airborne above them, and your shadow now shrinks and fades as you rise so it reads your height.

Fixed

  • The home-world camera no longer stutters or shakes while strafing; it now interpolates smoothly at any framerate, matching the in-run feel.
  • The Apprentice wand is now properly gripped in the right hand instead of floating beside the hip, and every character got rounder joints where limbs read jagged.
  • In default Drag to Look, holding either mouse button locks the pointer in place and hides it; releasing the final held button restores it. Classic Mouse Look remains an opt-in persistent lock.
  • The full-download build launches fullscreen. Web browsers may show their own brief pointer-lock guidance when a look drag begins.
2026-07-18 v0.3.0 from the release notes

THE WHOMP lands, the Hub grows into districts, and every world finally has its own sky.

Highlights

  • Ground weapon decals (toxic aura, faultline, gravity well, pulse mine, stomp and landing patches) render above grass again instead of getting punched through by a blade standing in front of them.
  • Run-map floor effect cues stay readable instead of getting lost under terrain drape.
  • The hub’s bottom-center interact card no longer sits under the boost dial after the dial’s move to the run-HUD anchor.
