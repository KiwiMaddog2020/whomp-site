Highlights

  • The E ability's hammer no longer clips back through its own caster in PvP; the head keeps its reach and the handle carries the shortening.
  • Whiteout's forcefield bubble is centred on her body instead of drifting to one side.

2026-08-14 v0.7.6 from the release notes

YOUR SAVE HAS BEEN RESET: one clean slate to fix two mountain maps that wore each other's names. Plus cleaner combat silhouettes and a softer hub soundtrack.

Highlights
