2026-11-01 v0.7.27 from the release notes

The hub loads faster, and menus and your first run load in the background

Highlights

  • The hub comes up with less to unpack, and the pause menu, results screen and run world load quietly in the background before you start a run, offline too.
  • The arcade's planet now appears as soon as playwhomp.com loads.
  • Clank, the Lamplighter, now runs the Dust shop at his glowing stall.
  • The title screen now loads about 60 KB less code before it appears, and the Settings panel looks and works exactly as before.

Fixed

  • Fixes and polish.
2026-10-31 v0.7.26 added under this version

Plasma Shield and Gravemaker hit bosses harder

Highlights

  • Plasma Shield now grinds bosses a little faster, and Gravemaker's second quake reaches a little farther, so both hit big targets harder.
  • The game now holds its framerate more steadily by lowering quality automatically when it dips.
  • The game has less to unpack before your first run; every hero looks exactly the same.
  • Once the Mine opens, its door no longer shows the heavy bar and lock across it.
2026-10-30 v0.7.26 added under this version

The game now makes a better first guess at your graphics settings by looking at your graphics card

Highlights

  • The game now makes a better first guess at your graphics settings by looking at your graphics card, your screen size and how smoothly the hub actually draws.
  • The Deep makes less garbage every frame, which smooths out small stutters.
  • New players now get a guided tutorial: the keeper walks you to Meadowfall and teaches the basics, and the old welcome card in the top right is gone.
  • Pressing B in the feedback screenshot editor now steps back to the form, and a second B closes the panel.
  • Small helper text on the title and settings is easier to read, and battle HUD labels no longer shrink below a readable size on phones.
2026-10-29 v0.7.26 added under this version

Save-format changes are now checked automatically

Highlights

  • Save-format changes are now checked automatically, so a save-shape change can no longer ship without its old-save test file.
  • Fire and lava fields cost less CPU when nothing is burning; they look exactly the same.
  • In the Forge's Trinkets tab the Slot, Unequip and Delete buttons now fit their words, and on a controller B backs out of a delete, LT and RT cycle the filters, and Y puts a trinket in Slot 2.
  • A controller player can now scroll the stats page and codex, browse the leaderboard and the tailor's rail, and file a report, with B closing each.
  • The title screen loads a little lighter: the cheats and labs panels now load the first time you open them.
2026-10-28 v0.7.26 added under this version

Trinkets can now be equipped, earned and used in runs

Highlights

  • Trinkets can now be equipped, earned and used in runs, with the WHOMP-TRINKETS cheat code to try them all.
  • Enemies in the Mine's big rooms now find their way to you through doorways instead of standing still.
  • The Eclipse Crown Starwyrm model is ready to ship behind its dev toggle.
  • Mine bosses now have a reward table ready: each boss chest pays a trinket by chance or Dust, and both get better the deeper you go.
  • Keyed Deep clears now open a Warden's chest of Dust and trinkets, a weekly chest waits by the Deep's door for last week's best clear, and any Deep clear pays a little Dust once a week.
