2026-09-28 v0.7.22 added under this version

Seven bosses fight with their own attacks, and the night skies get their moons back

Highlights

  • Seven bosses now fight with their own sets of attacks instead of sharing a handful of generic moves: the Oilfather, the Archivist, the Regent of Dis, the Grave Monarch, the Last Light, the Watcher on the Road and the Borealisk. The Tidebound seals have had their whole fight rebuilt too.
  • Three heroes got a glow-up: Grim Hex's hood now shows cloth at the front instead of a dark gap, Elder Star has real eyes with whites, an iris and an eyelid, and Orbital Mechanic's arms have a proper forearm, elbow and gauntlet, so his hands no longer sink into his suit while he moves.
  • Neon Necropolis, Void Rift and Sunken Sands have new night skies. Each moon now sits where that world's light comes from and reads as round instead of a tall oval. Neon Necropolis has misted spires under a crescent, Void Rift's sky is torn open with strange stars inside the tear, and Sunken Sands has a neon horizon under a striped arcade-cabinet moon.
  • Frostreach Pines, Shiverpeak Summit and The Ascent have new skies with the sun on the side the light comes from, sun dogs over Frostreach Pines and green curtains over The Ascent's glacier. Meadowfall, Glimmer Orchard and Mirage Flats now have soft, fluffy clouds with see-through edges.
  • The sky no longer slowly turns during a run: the sun and moon stay where each world's light comes from, from start to finish.
2026-09-26 v0.7.22 added under this version

The Fairy Forest river runs for everyone, and dictation works on the desktop

Highlights

  • In the Fairy Forest, enemies now wade into the river and cross it anywhere. Heroes and enemies move through the water at the same pace and the current pulls on both. The bridges are still the fast way across, nothing spawns in the water, and if the current carries you over the edge you land on a dry shelf and can walk back out.
  • Dictation in the bug and suggestion report works in the desktop app again: the app now lets the report panel use your microphone.
  • The travelling trader who wanders the maps looks the way it used to again.
  • Relic vending machines are now tinted by the rarity of what they are offering: blue for rare, purple for epic and gold for legendary.
  • The hub has a new sky, lit by a ringed planet, and Scorchdune, Gilded Canyon, Ember Wastes and Hellmouth have new skies with the sun on the side the light comes from.
2026-09-25 v0.7.22 added under this version

The crashed ship stands up, Fuse Wire's spark lands true

Highlights

  • The crashed ship in the hub now mends in stages as you bring ship cores home: the villagers keep the tear in her hull boarded up at first, and once your first core is back she stands upright and the boards come off.
  • Fuse Wire's spark now sits on the node that is actually burning instead of running ahead of it, and the unburnt cord keeps its glow until the fire reaches it.

  • In the Fairy Forest the lit path now runs unbroken past the spot where a run starts, and no path runs under a cottage any more.
2026-09-24 v0.7.22 added under this version

Weapons show their attack coming

Highlights

  • Weapons now show their attack coming before it lands. The Glass Cannon, Anvil, Ripcord, Sweepbeam, Flamethrower, Mortar, Sunpiercer, Shotgun, Groundbreaker, Photon Sword, Frost Lance, Static Lash, Arc Caster, Tether, Hailstorm, Chain Lightning, Beam, Spectral Arrows, Energy Bolt, Meteor Strike and Void Scythe each got a clearer tell. Damage is unchanged.
  • The Anvil now lifts overhead before each throw, a quarter second wind-up, and a strip on the ground shows where it will land while you lift. Its right click alt fire waits until the lift is done.
  • The Void Pulse Rifle and the Ripcord no longer show a held rifle: each is drawn as light at your aim, like the Shotgun, and both now wind up visibly before they fire. The Ripcord's drum is a ring of six chambers that spins up before each burst.
  • The desktop app now recovers from graphics crashes: if the graphics process crashes it relaunches on a safer graphics mode, and falls back to software rendering as a last resort.
  • The Glass Cannon's slug now draws at its true size, with a flare and ice spikes at the muzzle when it fires, and its shards take the core's ice colour.
2026-09-23 v0.7.22 added under this version

Your run stars, counted on the HUD

Highlights

  • Your run's stars are counted on the HUD: a small star count sits left of the stats readout, and every prompt to leave a run tells you how many stars are still out there.

  • The Ripcord now hits exactly where your cursor points, like the Void Pulse Rifle, and its tracer ends on what it hit.
