Bestiary

Every kind in the game, what it does, and where it turns up. Health, contact damage and XP are the values a kind starts a run with, before elapsed time, the level’s own multipliers and the opening grace period all scale them up, so read them against each other rather than as what you will meet at minute twelve.

Every value on this page is read from a verified generated artifact at build time, against game@d2b39395. The generator refuses stale artifacts, missing domains, missing entries and dead relations before it writes a page. Source: src/data/enemies.ts#ENEMY_KINDS. Optional director notes have 0 of 53 coverage, so these cards use canonical mechanics and authored registry copy only.

Every time on this page is minutes of real play. The game schedules on a clock that runs faster than the one you are watching, so these are converted: a slot written as 10:00 in the level tables lands about 6:00 into a real run. The conversion comes from the run mode’s own pace scale, and it holds up to the 20:00 mark where the pacing banks. Live-run speed is a base, not a final chase speed. The canonical policy says timed and per-instance multipliers still apply after the value shown here. Boss and miniboss health and damage are UNMEASURED. Runtime authority is private, multi-stage and mode-dependent, so the registry values for those two tiers are not published as encounter stats.

Canonical speed policy

Authored base and live-run base

entries.<id>.speed and refs.<id>.speedProfile.authoredBaseMps are the raw authored registry value. refs.<id>.speedProfile.liveRunBaseMps is the profile-gated base used by live-run consumers after src/data/enemies.ts#bandedEnemySpeed. Battle/Arena consumers retain the authored base; the registry is deliberately never rewritten by the band. liveRunBaseMps is before domains.enemies.scaling.speedPer50s and contextual/per-instance multipliers, so it is not an at-time chase speed. A null maxExclusiveMps with unbounded:true is the JSON-safe representation of the source table's Infinity ceiling.
