Wiki/World/Bestiary

Bestiary

What each kind does to you, and where you first run into it. Health, contact damage and kill XP are opening numbers that climb on three separate clocks while you play, so the sentence on a card outlives the bars underneath it.

Every time on this page is minutes of real play. The game schedules on a clock that runs faster than the one you are watching, so these are converted: a slot written as 10:00 in the level tables lands about 6:00 into a real run. The conversion comes from the run mode’s own pace scale, and it holds up to the 20:00 mark where the pacing banks. For basic and special kinds, the speed shown is a starting speed, not how fast the thing ends up chasing you: the run keeps multiplying it, and single instances carry their own multipliers on top. Bosses and minibosses carry no combat numbers here at all. What they have, how hard they hit and how they move is decided while the fight is running, in stages, and differently by mode, so the leftover values sitting in their rows would be a guess wearing the clothes of a fact.

Independent live-run clocks

Health, contact damage and kill XP scale separately

These are three different runtime steps, not one shared enemy-level curve.

Spawn healthEvery 25 seconds+ +7.5%
Contact damageEvery 30 seconds+ +10%
Kill XP rewardEvery 120 build-clock seconds+ +50%

Ordinary SpawnDirector wave health is resolved from its base before level/external multipliers and may also receive the mode-owned opening enemy HP bonus. Bosses, recurring minibosses, elites, set pieces and direct-spawn systems are excluded from that opening lever. Contact damage has its own elapsed-time step. Kill XP uses its separate build-clock step, then player and global XP multipliers apply.

Canonical speed policy

Authored base and live-run base

entries.<id>.speed and refs.<id>.speedProfile.authoredBaseMps are the raw authored registry value. refs.<id>.speedProfile.liveRunBaseMps is the profile-gated base used by live-run consumers after src/data/enemies.ts#bandedEnemySpeed. Battle/Arena consumers retain the authored base; the registry is deliberately never rewritten by the band. liveRunBaseMps is before domains.enemies.scaling.speedPer50s and contextual/per-instance multipliers, so it is not an at-time chase speed. A null maxExclusiveMps with unbounded:true is the JSON-safe representation of the source table's Infinity ceiling.
