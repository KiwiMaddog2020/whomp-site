Bestiary

Every kind in the game, what it does, and where it turns up. Health, contact damage and XP are the values a kind starts a run with, before elapsed time, the level’s own multipliers and the opening grace period all scale them up, so read them against each other rather than as what you will meet at minute twelve.

Every value on this page is read out of the game’s own registries at build time, from main@89d9d74f. Nothing here is retyped by hand, so it cannot fall behind a balance change. Bars compare an entry against the largest value in this roster, never an absolute ceiling, and the real number is always printed beside the bar. Written flavour notes are the director’s to write and 0 of 53 exist so far, so these cards are mechanics only.

Every time on this page is minutes of real play. The game schedules on a clock that runs faster than the one you are watching, so these are converted: a slot written as 10:00 in the level tables lands about 6:00 into a real run. The conversion comes from the run mode’s own pace scale, and it holds up to the 20:00 mark where the pacing banks. Speed is missing on purpose: the registry carries a speed for every kind, but basic and special kinds are re-banded before the game ever uses that number, and the banding is not in the data these pages read. Publishing the raw field would be publishing a speed nobody ever meets, so it is left out and said out loud instead. Boss and miniboss health and damage are missing for the same reason: the boss director replaces them at spawn from its own table, so the registry values for those two tiers are documentation rather than what you actually fight.
