Bestiary

Every kind in the game and where it turns up, with behavior and contextual stats only where the canonical runtime contract supports them. For basic and special enemies, health, contact damage and kill XP are starting values; each follows its own runtime scaling clock.

Canonical fields and measurements on this page are read from a verified generated artifact at build time, against game@0cb53bbe. Player-facing labels and explanations are either artifact semantics or source-audited editorial context; they never introduce an unsourced magnitude. The generator refuses stale artifacts, missing domains, missing entries and dead relations before it writes a page. Source: src/data/enemies.ts#ENEMY_KINDS. Optional director notes have 0 of 53 coverage, so these cards use canonical mechanics and authored registry copy only. This route also uses 53 game-owned visual associations from data/wiki-visuals.json (content 4d058131f50c38888083b2639cbc8f2eeea2687d922918f5319b91a8c28556ae, source f0e7a0e675f77795add2cc21b94b408486169add9d0fc5d7f1d20ab10ca6778b). Runtime portraits are deterministic isolated production renders in a neutral presentation context; they are not sprites, screenshots, live-world lighting, or mechanics evidence.

Every time on this page is minutes of real play. The game schedules on a clock that runs faster than the one you are watching, so these are converted: a slot written as 10:00 in the level tables lands about 6:00 into a real run. The conversion comes from the run mode’s own pace scale, and it holds up to the 20:00 mark where the pacing banks. For basic and special enemies, live-run speed is a base, not a final chase speed; timed and per-instance multipliers still apply. Boss and miniboss health, damage, behavior and final chase speed are UNMEASURED. Their director authority is private, multi-stage and mode-dependent, so registry placeholders and partial bases are not published as encounter facts.

Independent live-run clocks

Health, contact damage and kill XP scale separately

These are three different runtime steps, not one shared enemy-level curve.

Spawn healthEvery 25 seconds+ +7.5%
Contact damageEvery 30 seconds+ +10%
Kill XP rewardEvery 120 build-clock seconds+ +50%

Ordinary SpawnDirector wave health is resolved from its base before level/external multipliers and may also receive the mode-owned opening enemy HP bonus. Bosses, recurring minibosses, elites, set pieces and direct-spawn systems are excluded from that opening lever. Contact damage has its own elapsed-time step. Kill XP uses its separate build-clock step, then player and global XP multipliers apply.

Canonical speed policy

Authored base and live-run base

entries.<id>.speed and refs.<id>.speedProfile.authoredBaseMps are the raw authored registry value. refs.<id>.speedProfile.liveRunBaseMps is the profile-gated base used by live-run consumers after src/data/enemies.ts#bandedEnemySpeed. Battle/Arena consumers retain the authored base; the registry is deliberately never rewritten by the band. liveRunBaseMps is before domains.enemies.scaling.speedPer50s and contextual/per-instance multipliers, so it is not an at-time chase speed. A null maxExclusiveMps with unbounded:true is the JSON-safe representation of the source table's Infinity ceiling.
