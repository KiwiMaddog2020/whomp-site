Built in the open

WHOMP is a 3D horde-survivor built by one person and a crew of AI agents, shipping several times a day to a small circle of testers. The unusual part is not the speed. It is that the development log below is generated straight from the repository, so it reports what actually shipped rather than what we meant to ship, including the fixes that were embarrassing.

Almost no studio publishes its real defect ledger. We do, because the ledger is the most honest thing we have and it is the reason the game gets better this fast.
