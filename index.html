What a run does to you

20 minutes, one weapon you aim yourself, and a horde that keeps finding out the ground is optional. The final horde turns up at 18:00 and does not thin out, because thinning out is not what it does. Hold it for 2 minutes, bank at 20:00, and then walk away clean, or stay in and find out how much worse it gets.

Nothing to download, nothing to install, and nobody here wants your email address. Click it and you are already being chased.

Everything currently in there with you, hostile and otherwise.

13 worlds 58 enemies 11 characters 9 aimed cores 38 weapons

Your kit

Five things go in with you, and you aim exactly one of them. Every level up offers 3 more, you keep one, and the other 2 are gone for good.

One of 9Aimed

THE CORE

The one you aim.

You pick a core at the door, and it holds a slot of its own that the draft cannot reach. It is the one weapon in the run that waits for you to point it.

4 slotsAutomatic

THE ARSENAL

4 that fire themselves.

Room for 4, drawn from 38 weapons that keep their own time and never ask you for permission. 8 of them have an end form, and it only arrives if you max the weapon, carry its paired tome, and open a boss chest.

4 slotsPassive

THE TOMES

4 that bend the math.

4 more slots, filled out of 17 tomes. None of them fires at anything, and every one of them changes what the rest of your build is worth.

Every 50 secondsOn Q

THE WHOMP

The button the game is named after.

You come down on Q and the ground does the arguing. It is yours from the first second of the run, and it comes back every 50 seconds before anything you are carrying cuts into that.

One of 11Picked first

YOUR CHARACTER

Each one cheats differently.

11 to choose from, all of them open on a fresh save. Every one carries a rule that is always on and a signature move nobody else gets.
