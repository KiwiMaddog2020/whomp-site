Measured builds

300 level-4 pairs were measured exhaustively on the smaller 12-seed cohort. Pair output, spread and synergy are shown on both axes; the 4 featured build chains are measured extensions, not hand-picked recommendations.

Every value on this page is read from a verified generated artifact at build time, against game@d2b39395. The generator refuses stale artifacts, missing domains, missing entries and dead relations before it writes a page. Source: data/tier-rankings.json · fingerprint 869e0ddba75a8334eb2a8850c45bece3.

Pair and build samples use 12 seeds, not the 40-seed single-weapon sweep. A weapon's mean synergy across every pair it appears in, beside its own solo output. On the trash axis these are ANTI-CORRELATED: the best partners are the weakest solo weapons, because two strong AoE weapons overkill the same 35-HP target while a weak single-target weapon adds throughput it does not have to share. The DIRECTION is a property of how damage stacks; the MAGNITUDE belongs to this fixture, which holds trash HP fixed where a real run scales it.
