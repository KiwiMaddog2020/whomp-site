Wiki/Buildcraft/Tomes

Tomes

A tome raises one number and keeps raising it every time you take it again. Nothing here fires, aims or lands: this is the half of a build that quietly makes the other half bigger, and six of them are also the key to a weapon end form.

No tome has a letter grade here. A tome is worth whatever the build around it is worth, and nobody has picked the build to measure it against yet, so putting these in order would be inventing a fact rather than reporting one. What each level actually adds is on the card, and you can do the comparing.
